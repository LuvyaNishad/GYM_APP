# Additional Gym Exercise Library --- Biceps, Forearms and Back

This is a **supplementary Markdown file** for `gym_exercise_library.md`.
It adds further biceps, forearm/grip, lat, mid-back, lower-back,
trapezius, and scapular-control exercises.

> **Duplicate policy:** Common exercise names already in the original
> library are omitted. Some entries are variations of known movements
> using a different grip, setup, or equipment; they are labelled as
> variations, not entirely different muscle actions.

Each entry includes an ID, description, primary/secondary muscles,
equipment, difficulty, tags, instructions, safety notes, and logging
suggestions.

## Biceps and Brachialis

### Zottman Curl

-   **ID:** `zottman-curl`
-   **Description:** A biceps and brachialis movement or variation for
    training the listed muscles.
-   **Primary muscles:** Biceps brachii
-   **Secondary muscles / stabilizers:** Brachialis; brachioradialis
-   **Equipment:** Dumbbells
-   **Difficulty:** Intermediate
-   **Tags:** `biceps`, `forearms`, `rotation`,
    `exercise-library-addition`
-   **How to perform:** Curl with palms up, rotate palms down at the
    top, and lower slowly.
-   **Coaching / safety notes:** Use light weights and keep elbows
    still.
-   **Logging suggestions:** Record sets, reps, load, and rest; for
    holds/carries, record duration or distance.
